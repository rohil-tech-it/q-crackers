Stay Safe

Fireworks Safety Tips

Celebrate responsibly with these essential safety guidelines

Your Safety is
Our Priority

Maintain Distance

Keep at least 15 feet away from lit fireworks. Never lean over or stand directly above any firework.

Keep Water Nearby

Always have a bucket of water or fire extinguisher ready to handle any unexpected situations instantly.

Adult Supervision

Children must always be supervised by adults. Never allow kids to handle fireworks independently.

Never Relight a Dud

Wait 10 minutes, then soak in water. Never attempt to relight or handle a firework that didn't ignite.

Proper Storage

Store fireworks in a cool, dry place away from heat sources, electrical appliances, and flammable materials.

Wear Protective Clothing

Avoid loose or synthetic clothing. Wear cotton clothes for better protection while handling fireworks.
